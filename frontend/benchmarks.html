The 10 Codified Safe Benchmark Clauses

Every incoming contract clause is embedded with pgvector and checked for compliance against these explicit commercial standards.

High Stake Priority
1. Limitation of Liability
Cap: 12 Mo Fees

Benchmark Standard: Aggregate liability must not exceed total fees paid by Customer in the preceding 12 months, with mutual waivers of consequential and punitive damages under UCC § 2-719.

"Neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the total amount paid in the preceding 12 months, excluding breaches of confidentiality or gross negligence."

High Stake Priority
2. Mutual Indemnification
Mutual & IP Safe

Benchmark Standard: Indemnification must be mutual and strictly limited to third-party claims arising from material breach, gross negligence, or intellectual property infringement.

"Each party shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims arising from material breach or IP infringement."
Test Indemnity in Sandbox
Medium Stake
3. Termination for Convenience
30-Day Notice

Benchmark Standard: Mutual right to terminate for convenience upon at least 30 days prior written notice, and termination for cause upon 30 days failure to cure material breach.

"Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon giving at least thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party."
Test Termination in Sandbox
Medium Stake
4. Auto-Renewal Notice Window
60-Day Window

Benchmark Standard: Non-renewal notices must be permissible via electronic mail up to 60 days prior to term expiration, preventing multi-year evergreen lock-in.

"Agreement shall automatically renew only if neither party provides written non-renewal notice at least sixty (60) days prior to term end."
Test Auto-Renewal in Sandbox
Regulatory Priority
5. Data Privacy & Incident Notice
24h Incident SLA

Benchmark Standard: GDPR Article 28 compliance, 24-hour mandatory written security breach notification, and explicit prohibition against commercializing customer telemetry.

"Vendor shall maintain strict GDPR Article 28 safeguards, notifying Customer within twenty-four (24) hours of any confirmed security incident."
Test Data Privacy in Sandbox
High Stake Priority
6. Deliverable IP Ownership
Work for Hire

Benchmark Standard: Custom deliverables, configurations, and customer data remain 100% owned by the enterprise upon payment, with royalty-free embedded vendor licenses.

"Customer shall own all right, title, and copyright in custom deliverables as a work-made-for-hire, with Vendor retaining background tools."
Test IP Ownership in Sandbox
Standard Operational
7. Net 30 Payment Terms
Net 30 Days

Benchmark Standard: Undisputed fees payable Net 30 days from formal invoice receipt, with explicit right to withhold disputed amounts in good faith without interest penalties.

"Customer shall pay undisputed invoices Net 30 days from receipt, retaining right to dispute billed charges in good faith prior to due date."
Test Payment Terms in Sandbox
Standard Operational
8. Confidentiality Survival
3-Yr Survival

Benchmark Standard: Mutual confidentiality protections surviving at least 3 years post-termination, with trade secrets and source code surviving indefinitely.

"Confidentiality obligations shall survive for three (3) years post termination, with trade secrets surviving until no longer secret under law."

Jurisdictional Priority
9. Delaware Governing Law
DE Chancery Court

Benchmark Standard: Exclusive venue in Delaware State or Federal courts without regard to conflict of laws, aligning with predictable commercial litigation standards.

"This Agreement shall be governed by Delaware law, and parties consent to exclusive jurisdiction in state or federal courts located in Wilmington, DE."

Standard Operational
10. Express Warranties & SLAs
90-Day Warranty

Benchmark Standard: 90-day express performance warranty against documented specifications, 99.9% uptime SLA commitments, and guarantees of malware-free delivery.

"Vendor expressly warrants services shall perform substantially in conformance with specifications for ninety (90) days and contain zero malicious code."
