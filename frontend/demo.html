Engine Architecture

How 1-Click Redline Analysis Operates

Clausify connects semantic vector retrieval directly to codified statutory standards, delivering transparent, verifiable legal redlines in milliseconds.

1
Deterministic Span Extraction

Incoming contract clauses are tokenized and parsed against exact sentence boundaries. No text is hallucinated or modified prior to statutory rule matching.

2
Statutory Benchmark Scoring

The clause is benchmarked against UCC Section 2-719, Delaware Chancery precedents, and GDPR Article 33 to compute an objective risk score from 0 to 100.

3
Surgical Redline Synthesis

Based on your chosen negotiation posture (Firm Counsel, Neutral, or Aggressive), the engine synthesizes minimal surgical diffs ready for counterparty circulation.
